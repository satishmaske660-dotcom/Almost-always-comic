A vertical-reading adaptation of Almost, Always, preserving the story's quiet gestures, conversations and emotional turns. Rohan and Tara keep the same facial identities throughout; their clothes change naturally with each scene and point in the timeline.

All 14 chapters are sourced from the original book website. The reader currently contains preview adaptations for selected chapters; the remaining chapters link to their original text while their webtoon scenes and artwork are developed. No story events will be invented.
